title: Capturing Races (Semeai)
tags: [middlegame, capturing_race, semeai, liberties]
---
A capturing race (semeai) is when two groups that cannot make two eyes are fighting to capture each other first. The side with more outside liberties usually wins, but only if they play correctly. The key question is always: how many liberties do I have, and how many does my opponent have? Count them before you play, not while you play.

Three rules cover most races. First, fill your opponent's outside liberties before your own — every move that reduces their count is worth one liberty; every move that reduces your own count is a liberty you lose. Second, if there is a shared liberty (a point that counts for both groups), the side that has to fill it is at a disadvantage, so avoid filling shared liberties until necessary. Third, if one side has an eye and the other does not, the side with the eye wins most races of equal liberties, because the eye space can't be filled. "Eye beats no-eye" and "outside first, shared last" are the two mantras.
