A ponnuki is the diamond of four friendly stones that remains after capturing a single enemy stone in the centre. The proverb values a ponnuki at "thirty points" of influence in open positions because it radiates power in four directions, has perfect eye shape potential, and is extremely hard to attack.

Its value is contextual. A ponnuki on the second line or jammed against opposing thickness is worth far less than one facing an open area. When KataGo rewards a sequence that produces a ponnuki, the reason is usually the central influence it generates, not the single captured stone.
