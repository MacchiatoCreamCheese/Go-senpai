title: Direction of Play
tags: [strategy, whole_board, opening]
---
Direction of play is the question of which side of a stone, wall, or weak group is the biggest — not in points, but in potential and in relation to the rest of the board. It is the single most common strategic error in amateur games: playing the right kind of move on the wrong side.

The classic rule is "play away from strength, toward weakness": extending from a wall into an open area builds a framework, while extending toward an opponent's thickness wastes the stones. When KataGo's top move is a seemingly normal extension on the other side of the board, the lesson is almost always direction.
