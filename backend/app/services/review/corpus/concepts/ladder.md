---
id: ladder
title: Ladder (Shicho)
tags: [tactics, capture, reading]
---
A ladder is a sequence of ataris that drives a stone or group diagonally across the board until it hits the edge or a friendly "ladder breaker" stone. A ladder that reaches the edge without running into supporting stones captures; a ladder that runs into a supporting stone fails, and the escaping stones usually become a disaster for the player who started the ladder.

Always read ladders before starting one, and always check whether the opponent has a ladder breaker anywhere on the line. KataGo's sharpest score drops in the opening often come from playing a move that only works "if the ladder works," when in fact a stone three ladder-steps away refutes it.
