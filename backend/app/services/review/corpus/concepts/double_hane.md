id: double_hane
title: Double Hane
tags: [tactics, tesuji, fighting]
---
A double hane is a second hane played immediately after the opponent answers your first hane with their own hane. It deliberately creates a cutting point on your own stones in exchange for an even bigger cutting point on the opponent's stones, usually because your side has more support.

The double hane is a fighting tesuji — it commits to a local fight and only works if you can win the resulting capturing race or cut. Avoid it when you are thin nearby or when the opponent has a ready ladder; choose it when KataGo's top move cuts sharply and your stones have backup.
