---
id: snapback
title: Snapback
tags: [tactics, capture, tesuji]
---
A snapback is a tactic that sacrifices one stone so that when the opponent captures it, the capturing stones are immediately in atari and can be recaptured — netting the sacrificed stone plus whatever the opponent had. It is one of the most common tesuji motifs in tsumego and shows up in almost every game above single-digit kyu.

Watch for snapbacks whenever the opponent has a group with a single liberty inside your shape. If you played a quiet move when a snapback was available, KataGo will usually flag it as a major blunder because the snapback is often the difference between killing a group and letting it live.
