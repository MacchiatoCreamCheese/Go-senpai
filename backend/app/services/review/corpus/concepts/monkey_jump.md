id: monkey_jump
title: Monkey Jump
tags: [endgame, tesuji, territory]
---
A monkey jump is a large knight's move from a second-line stone down to the first line, reaching deep into the opponent's territory along the edge. It is one of the biggest standard endgame tesuji — typically 8–12 points depending on the position — and it is almost always sente because the opponent must block to prevent a further reduction.

The defender's correct response is usually a diagonal block that seals the corner at minimum cost; a wrong block can double the loss. If the opponent left a monkey jump open and you played a smaller endgame move instead, KataGo will likely flag it as one of the bigger endgame mistakes in the game.
