---
id: damezumari
title: Damezumari (Shortage of Liberties)
tags: [tactics, liberties, reading]
---
Damezumari is the condition of being unable to play a move you would otherwise want to play because doing so would put your own stones in self-atari or reduce your liberties below the opponent's in a capturing race. It is the single most common reason a "reading mistake" turns into a capture.

When counting liberties, always count the opponent's response moves too. If your plan requires three tempi but you only have two liberties, the plan fails to damezumari. KataGo often highlights damezumari positions as turning points because the losing side had a way out if they had noticed the liberty count.
