---
id: dont_push_from_behind
title: Don't Push from Behind
tags: [strategy, proverb, shape]
---
The proverb "don't push from behind" warns against a sequence where you play a series of contact moves along a line while the opponent simply extends — with each exchange, the opponent's wall grows taller and yours stays one line lower. You are building their territory and influence for free.

The fix is to jump, hane, or tenuki instead of continuing to push. When KataGo marks three or four consecutive small losses in the opening, the underlying pattern is very often a push-from-behind sequence that should have been broken off after the first move.
