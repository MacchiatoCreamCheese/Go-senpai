---
id: tigers_mouth
title: Tiger's Mouth
tags: [shape, connection, eye_shape]
---
A tiger's mouth is three friendly stones arranged around a single empty point so that any enemy stone played into that point is immediately in atari. It makes a half-eye, threatens capture, and is the standard way to defend a cutting point without playing the heavy solid connection.

Prefer a tiger's mouth over a solid connection when the extra liberty and eye-shape potential matter, and over a hanging connection when the opponent has nearby stones that might exploit aji. It becomes weaker if the opponent can play a wedge that fills one of the mouth's supporting liberties.
