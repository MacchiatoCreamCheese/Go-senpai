id: crosscut
title: Crosscut
tags: [tactics, fighting, shape]
---
A crosscut occurs when both players cut each other at the same intersection, producing a four-stone interlocking pattern. The proverb says: "when crosscut, extend" — extending one of your two stones is usually correct because it strengthens your side of the fight and puts the most pressure on the opponent's cutting stone.

Atari is almost always wrong at a crosscut: it gives the opponent the choice of which side to save and hands them sente. A crosscut is often a signal that the local fight is about to decide a large area, so read carefully before choosing the extension direction.
