id: driving_tesuji
title: Driving Tesuji
tags: [tactics, tesuji, attack]
---
A driving tesuji is a move that forces the opponent to run in a direction that benefits you — usually toward your own thickness, where the chasing stones will either die or be forced to live small while you build outside. The essence is "attack for profit, not for the kill."

When you have a strong wall and the opponent has a weak group nearby, the correct move is almost never a direct attempt to kill; it is a capping or driving move that converts the attack into territory. A common error is trying to kill directly and letting the opponent live inside, erasing your thickness.
