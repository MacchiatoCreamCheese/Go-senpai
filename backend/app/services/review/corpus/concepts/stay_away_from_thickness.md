---
id: stay_away_from_thickness
title: Stay Away from Thickness
tags: [opening, strategy, proverb]
---
The proverb "stay away from thickness — your own or the opponent's" says that playing close to any strong wall is inefficient. Close to the opponent's thickness your stones have no room to make shape; close to your own thickness you duplicate influence you already have, wasting a move.

The standard extension distance from a wall is "two lines for each stone of the wall, then add one" — so a three-stone wall wants an extension around seven points away. If KataGo's top move is a distant extension and you played next to your own wall, the correction is almost always this principle.
