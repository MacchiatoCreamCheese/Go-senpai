title: Bent Four in the Corner
tags: [endgame, life_and_death, corner]
---
Bent four in the corner is a specific corner shape that, under most rulesets, is considered dead as-it-stands even though it appears to have two eyes locally — because the attacker can create a ko at their leisure later, and the defender cannot win that ko if the attacker has removed all ko threats first. Under Japanese rules it is conventionally dead without play; under Chinese rules the status depends on actual ko threats.

This is a corner-specific status; the same shape is not automatically dead elsewhere. In a real game, recognising bent four means not wasting moves "killing" a group that is already dead, and not feeling safe with a group that only has bent-four eye shape.
