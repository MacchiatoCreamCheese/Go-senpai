id: thickness_vs_territory
title: Thickness vs Territory
tags: [strategy, whole_board, influence]
---
Thickness is a strong, settled wall or group facing an open area; territory is the ground already enclosed. The two are meant to balance: thickness is not itself territory, and its value must be realised by attacking weak stones or building a framework from it.

The proverbs "don't use thickness to make territory" and "stay away from thickness — your own or the opponent's" both describe the error where a player cashes thickness directly into small profit, or lets the opponent play close to it. When KataGo prefers a distant extension over a local territorial move, the reason is usually that the local move under-uses thickness.
