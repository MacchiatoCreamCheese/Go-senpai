---
id: miai
title: Miai
tags: [strategy, shape, life_and_death]
---
Two points are miai if playing either of them gives the same result: if the opponent takes one, you take the other. Miai reasoning is fundamental to life and death (two separate eye spaces can be miai for life) and to strategy (two big points can be miai for whole-board balance).

A common mistake is to play one of two miai points when both are still open, wasting a tempo — the opponent simply plays the other. Recognising miai tells you the move is not urgent; you can tenuki to a truly unique big point and take the remaining miai later.
