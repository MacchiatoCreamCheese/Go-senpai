---
id: sente_gote
title: Sente and Gote
tags: [strategy, endgame, tempo]
---
A move is sente if the opponent must answer it locally (or suffer a larger loss); it is gote if the opponent can tenuki without consequence. In the endgame, sente moves are vastly more valuable than their raw point count suggests because the player who ends a local sequence in sente keeps the initiative.

The endgame proverb "double sente first, then your sente, then their sente, then gote" orders endgame moves correctly. A common KataGo-flagged error is playing a larger gote move before a smaller sente one — the follow-up to the sente is what makes the overall swing bigger.
