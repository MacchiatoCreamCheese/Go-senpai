id: corner_side_center
title: Corner Before Side Before Centre
tags: [opening, fundamentals, proverb]
---
The opening proverb "corner first, then side, then centre" reflects the raw efficiency of making territory: a corner needs roughly six stones to enclose, a side about ten, and a centre area many more. In the very first moves of the game, playing a corner point is almost always more efficient than a side point, and a side point is more efficient than a centre point.

The proverb breaks down later in the game and in modern AI-influenced openings, where 3-3 invasions, early shoulder hits, and high-centre strategies have become normal. But at the first few moves of a 19×19 game, ignoring a free corner to play on the side or centre is one of the clearest opening errors KataGo will flag.
