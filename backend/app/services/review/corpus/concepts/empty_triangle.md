---
id: empty_triangle
title: Empty Triangle
tags: [shape, bad_shape, local]
---
An empty triangle is three friendly stones in an L-shape (two stones plus one diagonally adjacent stone that shares both their liberties) with no enemy stone filling the diagonal. It is the archetypal bad shape: it gives only four liberties where a bent-four or diagonal extension would give five, and it wastes a move on a point that already had an influence. The Go proverb says "don't make empty triangles."

It is excusable (and sometimes correct) when the shape captures stones, defends a cutting point, or makes eye shape that would otherwise be missing. Outside those cases, prefer a diagonal, a kosumi, or a one-point jump that achieves the same connection with better shape.
