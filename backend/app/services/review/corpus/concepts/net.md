---
id: net
title: Net (Geta)
tags: [tactics, capture]
---
A net is a move that traps an enemy stone by cutting off its escape squares without giving atari. Unlike a ladder, a net does not depend on the whole board — it captures locally, is immune to ladder breakers, and leaves the captured stone with aji that is usually favourable to the surrounding player.

When a ladder fails or is risky, look for a net first. Typical net shapes are a knight's move or a one-point jump placed so that both escape directions for the enemy stone are covered.
