---
id: reduction_vs_invasion
title: Reduction vs Invasion
tags: [strategy, moyo, decision]
---
Against a large opponent framework you have two broad options: invade (play inside and try to live) or reduce (play on the border and limit the framework's maximum size). Invasion wins more when it succeeds but loses more when the invading group dies; reduction is safer but concedes some territory.

The rough rule is: if the framework is not yet sealed and a reasonable invasion point exists, invade; if the framework is solid and a deep invasion would likely die, reduce. KataGo often prefers a shoulder-hit or capping reduction where the amateur instinct is a deep invasion — the reduction gains in safety what it gives up in maximum points.
