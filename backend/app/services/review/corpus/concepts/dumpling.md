id: dumpling
title: Dumpling
tags: [shape, bad_shape]
---
A dumpling is a solid lump of four or more friendly stones bunched together without any shape — no eye potential, no direction, and every stone sharing liberties with its neighbours. The proverb warns: "don't make dumplings."

Dumplings usually arise when a player keeps extending in atari, capturing a stone in the wrong direction, or grinding out a short-term gain at the cost of shape. KataGo flags these sequences because each added stone contributes far less than it should to real estate or attack; the right move is usually to sacrifice, tenuki, or cut in a different direction.
