---
id: sabaki
title: Sabaki (Flexible Settling)
tags: [strategy, invasion, shape]
---
Sabaki is the art of settling a weak or invading group in the opponent's sphere of influence by playing flexibly — using contact moves, sacrifices, and forcing exchanges to build shape quickly rather than trying to run out into the centre. The goal is not to win the local fight but to end with a living, resilient group that has spent few stones.

Typical sabaki techniques are attachment, crosscut, and the double hane. When the opponent has you surrounded and KataGo's top move is a counter-intuitive attachment on a strong stone, the idea is almost always sabaki: provoke an exchange, sacrifice one or two stones, and live with the rest.
