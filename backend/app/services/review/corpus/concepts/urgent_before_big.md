id: urgent_before_big
title: Urgent Points Before Big Points
tags: [strategy, proverb, tempo]
---
The proverb "urgent points before big points" says that a move that settles the life and death of a group, or prevents a key attacking move against your weak stones, should be played before any move that simply takes territory — no matter how large the territory looks.

The test is: if the opponent plays here next, does one of my groups become weak or does theirs become strong? If yes, it is urgent. A large endgame-style move that leaves a weak group behind is almost always a KataGo-flagged mistake, because the follow-up attack costs more than the big move was worth.
