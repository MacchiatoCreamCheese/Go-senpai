title: Table Shape
tags: [shape, good_shape, eye_shape]
---
Table shape is four friendly stones forming a 2×2 square with one corner missing — a compact "table" of stones. It is efficient shape: it develops quickly, makes an eye easily, and is hard to attack because the missing corner is surrounded by three stones' influence.

It often arises naturally from a knight's move followed by an attachment or from a 3-3 invasion sequence. Recognising table shape helps you choose between two otherwise equivalent-looking moves — the one that makes the table is usually stronger.
