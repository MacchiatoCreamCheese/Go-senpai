---
id: bamboo_joint
title: Bamboo Joint
tags: [shape, connection]
---
A bamboo joint is two pairs of friendly stones placed two points apart so that any cut by the opponent can be answered by filling on the other side. It is a solid, unconditionally connected shape and is one of the cleanest ways to link two groups together.

Use a bamboo joint when a direct solid connection would be slow or heavy but a diagonal might be cut. It is usually better than an empty triangle for connecting because it covers two liberties and keeps both stones active.
