---
id: placement
title: Placement Tesuji (Oki)
tags: [tactics, tesuji, life_and_death]
---
A placement tesuji is a stone played inside the opponent's shape — not connected to any friendly stone — that destroys eye space or breaks a vital point. Placements are the signature move of life-and-death problems and often look "impossible" because they appear to be self-atari or simply isolated.

The classic case is the 2-1 vital point inside a corner shape: a single placement prevents the corner from making two eyes. When KataGo plays what looks like an unsupported stone deep in the opponent's territory, the reason is usually a placement that reduces eye count by one.
