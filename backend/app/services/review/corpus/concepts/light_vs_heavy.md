---
id: light_vs_heavy
title: Light vs Heavy Stones
tags: [strategy, shape, invasion]
---
A light group is one that can be sacrificed in part without losing the whole: it has flexible shape, multiple escape routes, and is spread out enough that losing two or three stones is survivable. A heavy group is the opposite — too many stones clumped together to abandon and too few liberties or eye shape to live easily.

The danger of heavy play is that the attacker gains double value by attacking: profit on the outside while the heavy group has to run. Inside opponent territory or when invading, play lightly — jump, cap, and be willing to sacrifice — rather than trying to save every stone.
